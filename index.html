Straight answers

The questions we get asked first.

Is AEO just SEO with a new name?

It shares the foundation and breaks from it at the output. SEO optimises for a ranked list of links. AEO optimises for being named and quoted inside a generated answer, which depends more on entity clarity, source quality and third-party corroboration than on position.

Can you guarantee we appear in ChatGPT?

No, and anyone who does is selling you something. Models are probabilistic and change without notice. What we commit to is a measured baseline, a fix list tied to how these systems select sources, and monthly reporting on movement.

Do we have to stop our SEO or paid programme?

No. AEO sits on top of them. Most of our clients run AEO, SEO and paid together under one team, so the citation work, the landing pages and the ad spend are not pulling against each other.

How soon does anything move?

Entity and schema corrections can show up in answers within weeks. Displacing an established competitor on high-intent queries is a quarter of sustained work. Paid placements move immediately, which is why we usually run both.

Who is this for?

Brands with real revenue to protect: ecommerce, B2B, education and finance, typically already spending on search and watching organic traffic flatten while the category keeps growing.
